# Titan · Part 2 of 3 · The Bang

## Narration (final)

A year before Titan imploded, people inside it heard a bang as loud as an explosion. They kept diving anyway.

This is part two of the Titan story.

July 2022. Titan is rising back up from the wreck of the Titanic. Suddenly, a huge bang, or cracking sound. A boat crew on the surface hears it too.

Titan's own sensors record it. A massive spike. And three of its eight sensors weren't even working.

Nobody takes the hull apart to look inside. Rush says it was probably just the sled banging against the frame.

Investigators later found the hull's strain readings had shifted after that dive. A sign of damage that nobody properly analyzed.

Then Titan spends seven months parked outside in Newfoundland. Uncovered, through snow and freezing cold. A cover would have cost seventeen hundred and fifty Canadian dollars. Never approved.

June 2023. Six days before the final dive, Titan is thrown and slammed against a steel platform with passengers inside. Rush tells them it's basically indestructible.

A passenger remembered him saying: "I'm going to get a dive in, even if it kills me."
