# Titan · Part 1 of 3 · The Warning

## Narration (final)

Five years before the Titan submarine imploded, one man tried to stop it. And he got fired for it.

This is part one of the Titan story.

January 2018. OceanGate's Director of Marine Operations, the man in charge of keeping passengers safe, inspects the company's new deep-sea submersible. His report lists twenty-six problems. His conclusion: until they're fixed, this sub should not dive.

He sits down with CEO Stockton Rush. Two hours. He argues for more testing of the hull.

Four days later, he's fired.

He files a whistleblower complaint. OceanGate sues him. An email about his case is sent to the Coast Guard, but it never reaches the right person. Worn down, he drops the complaint.

That same year, more than twenty deep-sea experts sign a letter begging Rush to follow industry safety practice. He gets it. Nothing fundamental changes.

Then, in 2019, a worker shines a flashlight behind the liner of the first hull, and finds a crack.

That hull was retired. A new one was built. And the warnings were about to get louder.

Part two: the bang that everyone heard. Follow so you don't miss it.
