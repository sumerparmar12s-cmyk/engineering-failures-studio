## Description
At 10:47 on the morning of June 18, 2023, a short text message reached a ship in the North Atlantic from more than two miles below the surface: "dropped two wts." Six seconds later, the Titan submersible sent its last signal — and then nothing. The man piloting it had designed it, sold the tickets, and been warned, in writing, by his own people, years before. So why did he go down anyway?

From a 2018 safety report that got its author fired, to a letter signed by more than twenty deep-sea experts, a crack found with a flashlight, a bang "as loud as an explosion" in 2022, seven months parked outside through a Newfoundland winter, and a passenger's memory of "I'm going to get a dive in, even if it kills me" — this is the full story of the Titan submersible, told the way the U.S. Coast Guard pieced it together.
