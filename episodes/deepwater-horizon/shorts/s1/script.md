# Short 1 — They sent the check home

## Narration (final)

They had one test that could check the cement at the bottom of an oil well. And they sent it home, to save a hundred and twenty-eight thousand dollars.

April twentieth, 2010. The Deepwater Horizon had just finished drilling a well, a mile under the Gulf of Mexico. A team of specialists was on board, with tools to check the cement plug: the cork holding back the oil and gas.

But the cement job looked fine on paper. So at eleven a.m., the team flew home on the helicopter.

That night, the cement failed. Oil and gas shot up the well, and the rig exploded. Eleven men were killed. And oil poured into the Gulf for eighty-seven days.

The full story of the last sixteen hours is on our channel. Would you have sent them home?
