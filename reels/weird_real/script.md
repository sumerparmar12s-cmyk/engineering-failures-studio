# Real footage that looks fake (Earth edition)

## Narration (final)

This bridge is made of steel. Watch what the wind does to it.

Washington State, 1940. Four months after it opened...

Then this.

The only life lost? A dog, trapped in a car.

In 1946, America dropped an atomic bomb on its own fleet. On purpose. Just to see what would happen.

And in 2018, the ground split open in a Hawaii neighborhood. Right across the street.

None of this is fake.

## Claim check
- Tacoma Narrows Bridge opened July 1, 1940; collapsed Nov 7, 1940 in ~42 mph wind. Only death: Tubby, a cocker spaniel left in Leonard Coatsworth's car. (Archive.org CEP176 description; WSDOT history)
- Operation Crossroads "Able", July 1, 1946, Bikini Atoll: B-29 dropped the bomb over a target fleet of surplus US and captured warships to test the effect on ships. Footage: US Army Signal Corps, NARA 111-ADC-7179 (public domain).
- Kilauea, May 8, 2018: fissure 13 opened and the spattering fissure cut across Leilani Street, Leilani Estates (USGS video description, public domain).
