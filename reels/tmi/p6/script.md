# Three Mile Island · Part 6 · The President Walks In

## Narration (final)

While experts still argued over whether the reactor could explode, the President of the United States walked straight into the damaged plant.

This is part six of Three Mile Island.

Sunday, April 1st, 1979. A helicopter lands near Three Mile Island. Out steps Jimmy Carter.

And Carter is not just any president. As a young Navy officer, he was picked by Admiral Hyman Rickover for the nuclear submarine program. He studied reactor technology and nuclear physics.

He even helped dismantle a damaged nuclear reactor in Canada, years before he became president.

Now he's back inside a nuclear plant in trouble. With him, First Lady Rosalynn Carter, Governor Dick Thornburgh, and the NRC's Harold Denton.

They walk into the Unit 2 control room. The President studies the panels himself.

His message to the country was simple: stay calm.

By the end of that weekend, the reactor was declared stable.

Two weeks later, Carter appointed a twelve-member Presidential Commission to find out exactly what went wrong.

But something strange had happened just before the accident. Something that made Americans even more afraid.
