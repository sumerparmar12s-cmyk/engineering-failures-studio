In 2026, the Pentagon released its UFO files to the public.

Even Apollo photos, with strange lights above the Moon.

But NASA checked this video. The object was two miles up, drifting with the wind. The speed was an illusion.

The rest? The Pentagon says: decide for yourself.

## Claim check
- "GO FAST" video: FLIR footage recorded by the aircrew of a U.S. Navy F/A-18F in 2015 (DoD/DVIDS description; archive.org dod-110886776).
- May 8, 2026: Pentagon began releasing UAP files on the PURSUE site (AP/ABC/OPB); officials said the public can draw its own conclusions. Release 1 contains NASA Apollo 12 (1969) and Apollo 17 (1972) photos with highlighted lights (files NASA-UAP-VM1..VM6).
- NASA UAP Independent Study Team (Sept 2023): GO FAST object at ~13,000 ft, moving ~40 mph, consistent with wind; apparent speed due to parallax.
