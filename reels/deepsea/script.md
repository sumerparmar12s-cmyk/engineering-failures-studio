# Deep-sea animals that look fake

## Narration (final)

This is a real animal. You can see right through it. And it's blinking at the camera.

A glass squid, twenty-three hundred feet under Alaska.

This octopus has ears like Dumbo. It flaps them to swim.

And this ghost octopus? It may be a species no one has ever named. Over two and a half miles down, near Hawaii.

Earth's real aliens live down here.

## Claim check
- Glass squid: NOAA Ocean Exploration, Seascape Alaska 3, Dive 01, 2023, ~700 m (2,300 ft); eye movements "thought to be the squid blinking" (NOAA caption).
- Dumbo octopus (cirrate): NOAA OER, Windows to the Deep 2019, off North Carolina, ~900 m. Dumbo octopuses swim by flapping their ear-like fins (NOAA).
- "Casper" ghostlike octopod: NOAA OER, 2016 Hohonu Moana, Feb 27, 2016, NE of Necker Island, Hawaii, ~4,290 m (2.67 mi); "almost certainly an undescribed species" (NOAA).
