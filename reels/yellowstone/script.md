# Yellowstone explosions

## Narration (final)

A Yellowstone webcam caught this pool... exploding.

It first blew up in 2024, and sent tourists running.

In June 2026, the ground blew open again. A brand-new pool appeared.

Why? Under the park sits a volcano, thirty by forty-five miles wide.

Scientists say a super-eruption isn't coming soon. But the steam? It can blow at any time.

## Claim check
- Black Diamond Pool, Biscuit Basin: hydrothermal explosion July 23, 2024 (visitors fled; boardwalk damaged); webcam eruption May 31, 2025 (USGS video).
- June 13, 2026: smaller hydrothermal explosion at Biscuit Basin; new thermal pool formed, spouting June 18, 2026 (USGS video).
- Yellowstone Caldera ~30 x 45 miles (USGS). USGS: super-eruption very unlikely in the foreseeable future; hydrothermal explosions are among the most likely hazards and can occur with little or no warning.
