# NASA photos that look fake

## Narration (final)

This isn't CGI. It's real video of a rover landing on Mars.

And these NASA photos look even more fake. They're not.

A face on Mars? Real photo, 1976. It's just a hill and some shadows.

A smiley face? That's a real crater on Mars.

A blue sunset? That's how the sun really sets on Mars.

And a giant hexagon on Saturn? A real jet stream, wider than Earth.

Space is stranger than fiction.
