# Strangest sounds ever recorded

## Narration (final)

In 1997, the ocean made this sound.

[PAUSE 1.8 bloop]

Heard over three thousand miles away. Some called it a monster.

The truth? Ice. A giant iceberg cracking apart.

But this sound has played since 1991.

[PAUSE 1.9 upsweep]

Scientists still don't know what makes it.

And this... is the wind on Mars.

[PAUSE 1.9 mars]

## Claim check
- "Bloop": NOAA PMEL, summer 1997; broad-spectrum sound consistent with icequakes from large icebergs cracking; icequakes detectable on sensors over 5,000 km (3,100 mi) apart. Audio sped up 16x (NOAA).
- "Upsweep": present since PMEL began recording in August 1991; origin unresolved (NOAA PMEL). Audio sped up 20x.
- Mars: first sounds recorded by Perseverance's microphone, Feb 20, 2021, faint wind (NASA/JPL-Caltech).
