# Tomorrow Island and Yesterday Island

## Narration (final)

These two islands are only two and a half miles apart. But they're almost a whole day apart.

This one is Russia, and it's already tomorrow. This one is America... still yesterday.

The International Date Line runs right between them. Locals call them Tomorrow Island and Yesterday Island.

So if you wave from here... you're waving into the future.

## Claim check
- Big Diomede (Russia) and Little Diomede (USA, Alaska), Bering Strait, about 2.4 mi (3.8 km) apart; the International Date Line runs between them.
- Time difference about 21 hours (Chukotka UTC+12 vs Alaska UTC-9; 20 h in Alaska summer time) -> "almost a whole day".
- Nicknames "Tomorrow Island" (Big Diomede) and "Yesterday Island" (Little Diomede) are widely used.
