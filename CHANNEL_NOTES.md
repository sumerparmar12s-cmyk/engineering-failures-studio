# Wrecklore — channel notes (creator preferences, keep for every new episode)

- English narration, US audience; faceless; ~10–12 min long videos (8+ min for mid-rolls).
- Every long video ALSO gets 2–3 Shorts (~45–60 s, 9:16, strong first-second hook, on-screen captions, loopable ending, CTA to the full video).
- Editing must feel NEW every time (different look, transitions, music, title style) — never reuse one template. Footage-first: AI video clips + real public-domain photos/docs (NTSB, NASA, CSB, etc.) with zooms, circles, arrows, animated maps; text only on top of footage, no text-only slides.
- Hook in the first seconds (shock + question), pattern interrupts every 40–60 s, myth vs fact, satisfying ending + CTA + 15 s end screen (no abrupt end).
- Sound: suspense music in suspense parts, typing/whoosh/impact SFX, narration pauses for key sounds; voice ≥ +10 dB above music+SFX.
- Thumbnails: realistic photo base, reference style (brown serif top word with dashes, big red serif word, ribbon), one real evidence inset.
- Descriptions: hook paragraph ending in a question → "From … to … this is the full story of …" → chapters → short credits → CTA. Never mention AI voice. Plain text when sending (not in code blocks), plus keywords, hashtags, location.
- Disclosure: tick "Altered or synthetic content: Yes" in Studio when AI/3D realistic scenes are used; one short on-screen note at the start ("Some scenes are AI visualizations"), not a tag on every shot (creator preference, to confirm).
- Deliver files under ~90 MB each (split long videos into parts) — big files have no download button in the app.
- Channel country is USA for now; switch to India right BEFORE applying to YPP (AdSense country must match residence).
